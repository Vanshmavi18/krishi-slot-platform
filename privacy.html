Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Last updated: September 2026 • Compliant with Indian Digital Data Protection Guidelines

Welcome to KrishiSlot, India's Unified Smart Mandi & APMC Procurement Platform. We are committed to protecting the personal information and agricultural transaction records of our farmers, buyers, and mandi staff.

1. Information We Collect

  • Mobile Number: Used strictly for secure SMS OTP authentication via Google Firebase and delivery of real-time mandi queue alerts.
  • Farmer Profile: Name, village, procurement records, vehicle registration, and Aadhaar/Bank details (securely masked) for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) settlement.
  • Slot Bookings: Crop selection, moisture assay, and arrival tokens.

2. Purpose of Data Usage

  • To authenticate and secure user logins using SMS OTP.
  • To transmit real-time arrival tokens, queue advancement notices, and payment confirmations via telecom SMS.
  • To prevent mandi congestion and ensure transparent fair-price procurement.

3. Security & Third-Party Services

Authentication is handled securely using Google Firebase Phone Authentication with automated reCAPTCHA spam protection. We do not sell or lease personal data to external advertisers.

4. Contact & Support

If you have any questions regarding your data or privacy, contact the KrishiSlot Mandi Administration at support@krishislot.gov.in.
